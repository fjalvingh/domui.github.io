# Look and feel

Everything that decides what a DomUI application looks like: the theme, the
stylesheets it is built from, and animation. Icons live with the components
that use them, under
[images, icons and file upload](../components/100-images-and-icons/index.md).

- [Themes](themes/index.md) - what a theme is, how one is named and selected,
  and how its files are found.
- [Overriding the theme](overriding-the-theme/index.md) - giving an application
  its own colours, fonts and metrics.
- [The winter theme](the-winter-theme/index.md) - the theme DomUI ships: how
  `style.scss` is put together and where each component is styled.
- [Styling a component](styling-your-component/index.md) - writing the SCSS for
  a component of your own, and the class naming that keeps it out of everyone
  else's way.
- [SASS/SCSS support](sass-scss-support/index.md) - the stylesheet pipeline, and
  passing values into a stylesheet from the outside.
- [Header contributors](header-contributors/index.md) - putting a stylesheet or
  a script in the page header.
- [Animations](animations/index.md) - the jQuery effects DomUI exposes.
